Supplements & Equipment

The basics every beginner should know about. This is general information, not medical advice. Ask a doctor before taking any supplement.

Basic Supplements

🫙

Whey Protein

An easy way to reach your daily protein goal when food alone is not enough.

More details

Take it after training or between meals. Check the label to see how much protein is in one scoop.

🍶

Creatine

A well-studied supplement that helps with strength and power during training.

More details

Usually taken once a day, every day. Read the label, and ask a doctor if you are unsure.

💊

Multivitamin

Helps fill small gaps in your diet. It does not replace real meals.

More details

Take it with a meal. It should support your diet, not replace it.

Basic Gym Equipment

||—||

Dumbbells

Useful for almost every exercise. Start light and add weight slowly.

More details

Start with a light weight so you can keep good form. Add weight a little at a time.

➰

Resistance Bands

Cheap, light, and easy to store. Good for warm-ups and home workouts.

More details

Bands come in different levels of tension. Start with the lightest one.

🗞️

Exercise Mat

Gives a soft, non-slip surface for floor exercises and stretching.

More details

Use it for stretching, core work, and floor exercises. Wipe it clean after use.
